# Tarot 13 — Style Guide

## Look
Glowing neon star maps on deep near-black, framed in fine gold filigree. Part planetarium, part antique astrolabe. Neon carries the energy; gold carries the sacred and precious.

**Gold rule:** gold is the accent, about 10–15% of any card. Neon stays dominant.

## Card frame
- Near-black background with a faint star field and soft nebula haze.
- Outer neon border line in the suit color, with a soft glow.
- Inner fine gold rule; gold filigree corners with star medallions.
- Card number at top in a small gold-ringed circle.
- Title at bottom in gold capitals (Cinzel or similar), the correspondence beneath in small spaced capitals in the suit color.

## Card art
- Each card's constellation is drawn as bright connected stars with neon lines.
- The brightest named star in each constellation is a **gold** point.
- Mythic figures are glowing line-art outlines, not full paintings.
- **Majors:** large gold planet or zodiac glyph behind the scene, gradient border.
- **Tens:** no figure, just the raw element as swirling neon energy.
- **Princesses:** their season's sky. **Princes/Queens/Kings:** luminous star-beings formed from their sign's constellation.

## Typography
- Titles: Cinzel (serif, wide letter-spacing), gold.
- Small labels: Jost or another clean geometric sans.

## Card back
- v1: `art/backs/card-back-v1.webp`. Central star in a gold zodiac wheel, crescents top and bottom, filigree corners.
- **Fixes needed:** remove the stray ♀ in the ring; check all 12 signs appear exactly once.
- **Reversals:** for a back that hides reversals, the design must look identical when rotated 180°. Flip the bottom crescent to open downward and replace glyphs with stars or dots.

## Print specs (typical)
- Tarot size is about 2.75 × 4.75 in (70 × 120 mm). Confirm with your printer.
- 300 DPI at final size, plus 1/8 in (3 mm) bleed on all sides.
- Keep text and key art inside a ~1/8 in safe margin.
