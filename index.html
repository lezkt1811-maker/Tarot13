How the shuffle works

Each shuffle style moves the cards its own way: the riffle splits and interleaves, the overhand drops packets, the wash swirls everything, the cut makes three piles, and the 13-pile deal deals and gathers piles. Every random choice comes from your browser's cryptographic random number generator.

Every style also includes a full, unbiased Fisher–Yates shuffle of all 78 cards, so whichever style you choose, the deck ends up completely random.

Each card has an equal chance of landing in every position. Your question, the spread, astrology, Ophiuchus and past readings never influence which cards appear, and there is no seed, weighting or re-rolling.

Cards are dealt from the top of the shuffled deck, so a spread never repeats a card. If you draw without shuffling, the full deck is shuffled once automatically first, and every new reading needs a fresh shuffle. The reading is written only after the cards are dealt.
