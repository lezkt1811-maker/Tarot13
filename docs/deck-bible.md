# The 13-Sign Celestial Tarot — Deck Bible

A 78-card tarot of the actual constellations

**Core architecture**

- 78 cards total
- 22 Major Arcana
- 56 Minor Arcana
- 4 suits: Wands, Cups, Swords, Pentacles
- 14 cards per suit
- Ophiuchus is the 9th sign
- Zodiac sequence:

♈ Aries → ♉ Taurus → ♊ Gemini → ♋ Cancer → ♌ Leo → ♍ Virgo → ♎ Libra → ♏ Scorpio → ⛎ Ophiuchus → ♐ Sagittarius → ♑ Capricorn → ♒ Aquarius → ♓ Pisces

The deck preserves the recognizable tarot structure while replacing the usual 12-sign framework with a 13-sign celestial system.

### ⛎ FIVE OF CUPS

*Ophiuchus • 2nd Scorpio Decan • Asclepius*

THE HEALER

This is the signature card of the entire deck.

**Theme:** Healing what was lost.

Ophiuchus is represented through Asclepius, the Serpent Bearer, whose mythology centers on medicine, healing and the restoration of life. The original Celestial Tarot explicitly assigns Five of Cups to Ophiuchus.

**Visual composition**

A central healing figure holds the serpent.

Behind her/him:

- a wounded vessel
- a restored vessel
- a serpent
- medicinal plants
- a celestial staff
- the constellation Ophiuchus
- Scorpio descending behind the figure
- Sagittarius rising beyond the horizon

The image should visually communicate:

Scorpio → Ophiuchus → Sagittarius

Death → Healing → Rebirth.

**Keywords**

healing • grief • regeneration • medicine • recovery • resurrection • transformation

**Upright**

Something damaged can be healed.

**Shadow**

Trying to resurrect something that has already completed its purpose.

**Core sentence**

“What was broken is not necessarily lost.”

## Vi. The Court System

The deck remains at 16 court cards, preserving the 78-card structure.

No extra Ophiuchus court.

**Pages**

Beginning • curiosity • learning

**Knights**

Movement • action • expression

**Queens**

Embodied mastery • receptivity • sovereignty

**Kings**

Directive mastery • authority • leadership

Ophiuchus therefore does not need a gimmicky “Ophiuchus King.”

Its power is much more interesting when it is structurally embedded in the Five of Cups.

- Celestial medicine
- Dream healing
- Chiron

**Color direction**

Black cosmic background with:

- electric cyan
- ultraviolet/violet
- hot magenta
- luminous white
- restrained metallic gold

No yellow/lime overload.

The serpent should feel celestial and intelligent, not horror-themed.

## Ix. The Deck'S Big Difference

This is not simply:

«“Celestial Tarot with Ophiuchus added.”»

It becomes:

A 13-sign tarot in which the actual constellation system becomes part of the tarot's narrative architecture.

The deck keeps the familiar tarot skeleton so someone who already knows tarot can pick it up immediately.

But underneath that familiar skeleton is a second system:

Tarot → Element → Sign → Decan → Constellation → Myth → Meaning

That layered structure is what makes the deck teachable.

## Sources and originality

The constellation and decan assignments are inherited from the *Celestial Tarot* architecture (Kay Steventon & Brian Clark). The 13-sign interpretation, themes, meanings and all artwork are original to this deck. The original deck uses 12 zodiac signs plus planets in the Majors and extra-zodiacal constellations in the Minors.

Ophiuchus doesn't need to be forced into the Major Arcana. It already has a fitting home in the Five of Cups: the card of grief becoming healing, sitting at the doorway between Scorpio and Sagittarius.
