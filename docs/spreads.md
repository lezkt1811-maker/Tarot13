# Tarot 13 — Spreads

All of these are built into the [shuffler](../shuffle.html).

## Tarot 13 spreads

These are the deck's beginner defaults, plus its two signature spreads.

### 1 card · What does the sky want me to see?
One card for a daily reading.

### 3 cards · Past → Present → Becoming
The classic three-card spread, with *Becoming* in place of *Future*: what is taking shape rather than what is fixed.

### 5 cards · The Serpent Path ♏ → ⛎ → ♐
The deck's signature spread, built on the Scorpio → Ophiuchus → Sagittarius movement: death, healing, rebirth.

1. **♏ What is ending**
2. **⛎ What needs healing** (the center card)
3. **♐ What is emerging**
4. What supports the healing
5. What resists the healing

Lay cards 1–3 in a row, then place 4 above and 5 below the center card.

```
            [4]
   [1]      [2]      [3]
            [5]
```

### 13 cards · The 13-Sign Wheel
One card for each sign, in sky order: Aries, Taurus, Gemini, Cancer, Leo, Virgo, Libra, Scorpio, **Ophiuchus**, Sagittarius, Capricorn, Aquarius, Pisces. Lay them in a circle.

## Classic spreads

| Cards | Spread | Positions |
|---|---|---|
| 2 | Situation · Advice | Situation → Advice. Also works as Me → Them, Problem → Solution, What I know → What I don't know |
| 3 | Situation · Challenge · Advice | Other versions: Mind → Body → Spirit, What → Why → What next |
| 4 | Situation → Outcome | Situation → Challenge → Action → Outcome |
| 5 | Decision | Situation · Option A · Option B · What you need to know · Likely direction |
| 6 | Path forward | Where you are · What brought you here · What is blocking you · What is helping you · What you should do · Where it leads |
| 7 | Horseshoe | Past · Present · Hidden influence · Obstacle · External influence · Advice · Outcome |
